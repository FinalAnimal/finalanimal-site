AUDIOVISUAL ARTIST — GALWAY, IRELAND

Conor Maloney

Work that sits between sound, image and ecology — immersive installation, audioreactive performance, and electronic instruments built from the ground up.

Final Animal is the practice of Conor Maloney, an audiovisual artist based in Galway, Ireland. The work moves between gallery installation, live performance and instrument design, treating those as one continuous material rather than separate disciplines.

A recurring method is scanning the natural world and rendering it as light. Point clouds — trees, landscapes, organisms captured by LiDAR — are processed and manipulated in real time, so that a subject can be both accurately recorded and visibly falling apart.

The instruments are built for the same reason. A laser harp made for SYNAESTHESIA plays audio and drives the visuals simultaneously, so there is no separation between the musician and the visual artist in performance. Each instrument is purpose-built for the context it plays in.
